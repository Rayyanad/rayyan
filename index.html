MY DAY JOB (WHEN I'M NOT SAVING THE CITY)

WORK EXPERIENCE

Product Management Intern, Risk Intelligence

Kinaxis

At Kinaxis, I'm working on Risk Intelligence, a product that helps global supply chain teams identify and respond to disruptions before they impact operations. I work closely with customers, engineering, and design teams to define AI-powered capabilities that transform external risk signals into actionable recommendations for planners. A big part of my role has been participating in client co-innovation initiatives, helping shape product direction based on direct customer feedback, and supporting sprint planning and initiative kickoffs to align teams around key priorities.

What I learned:

  • How to run client co-innovation sessions and turn customer feedback into product requirements.
  • How strategic initiatives are broken down into sprint plans and executable workstreams.
  • How to evaluate product decisions through both customer value and business impact.

Product Management Intern

Ampliwork

At Ampliwork, I worked on AI agents designed to automate property tax workflows for large accounting and consulting firms. I spent time understanding customer pain points, defining agent workflows, and helping build systems that could autonomously process documents, extract information, and reduce manual effort for tax professionals. Working in an early-stage startup meant being involved across product discovery, customer validation, and execution.

What I learned:

  • How to operate in a fast-moving 1→100 startup environment with limited resources.
  • How to validate AI products directly with customers before scaling development.
  • How to balance technical feasibility, customer needs, and business goals when prioritizing features.

Technology Consulting Intern

EY

At EY, I worked on a data transformation initiative for an oil and gas client, helping deliver a data asset management product that improved data ownership, governance, and discoverability across the organization. I collaborated with business and technical stakeholders to gather requirements, define product capabilities, and support implementation efforts that enabled teams to better manage and understand their data assets.

What I learned:

  • How to gather requirements from diverse stakeholders and translate them into product solutions.
  • How enterprise organizations approach data governance, ownership, and adoption.
  • How to drive alignment across business and technical teams on large-scale initiatives.

Analytics Intern

PepsiCo

At PepsiCo, I worked on demand analytics initiatives that helped improve forecasting and inventory planning across the business. By analyzing sales and demand trends, I supported decisions that ensured products like Lay's chips were available where customers expected them while minimizing inventory inefficiencies. My work helped connect analytical insights with real operational outcomes across the supply chain.

What I learned:

  • How forecasting models influence inventory and supply chain decisions at scale.
  • How to use data to identify trends and support business decision-making.
  • How to communicate analytical findings to stakeholders in a clear and actionable way.
